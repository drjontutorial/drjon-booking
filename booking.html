⚠️ Cancellation Policy

  • ⏰ 24-hour notice required — Lessons cancelled within 24 hours of the scheduled lesson time are non-refundable and will be charged in full.
  • 🎁 Two cancellation waivers per academic year — Each student has 2 cancellation waivers per academic year, from 1 September to 31 August.
  • ✅ After the 2 waivers have been used — Any cancellation within 24 hours will be charged as normal.
  • 🚫 No-show lessons — If a student does not attend the lesson without notice, the lesson will be charged in full. Waivers cannot be used for no-shows or cancellations made after the lesson starts.
